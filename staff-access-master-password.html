First-time setup

Create the owner account and set the master password. The master password is separate from your login password — it is required every time anyone deletes a bill or clears history.
Both passwords are stored only as one-way hashes in this browser's local storage. There is no recovery — keep them safe.

Staff Login

Each staff member logs in with their own credentials. What they can do is controlled by individually assigned permissions.
Deleting bills or history always requires the master password, regardless of who is logged in.
